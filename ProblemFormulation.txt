What is the central question?
Central question is to classify MRI Scans on the types of diseases ranging from L1 to L5. 

Why is it important in context?
It is important because it will fasten the examination process, prevent errors and help in early treatment.
Time Horizon:
Prediction occurs at the time of scan interpretation and supports clinical decision-making.

What would “success” look like?
Success as of now would like the model is learning well on training data and giving good results in terms of accuracy.

What variables are essential, and why?
The model should have enough data to learn well, identify patterns and easily understand what level of disease which mri scan represents.

Who will use the results?
Anyone working in medical field can use the results.

Is this realistic given constraints?
I think so it is possible, if we use proper methods and high end computing, although training on image data is a bit tricky and time consuming but we can make it happen
